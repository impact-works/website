## AI training and policy development with KRAC

*October 2026*

### Context

[Kootenay Regional Arts Council](https://www.kootenayartscouncil.com/) needed to understand what AI meant for its grant applications—and how to give applicants clear, fair guidance. Without a shared understanding of AI, staff were concerned that assessors’ fears about it could lead them to reject worthwhile projects simply because an applicant had used it to help write the application.

### What we did

We held preparation meetings with KRAC staff and researched other arts organizations’ AI policies. We then ran two 90-minute facilitated online workshops for staff and steering committee members: exploring AI tools, discussing their use in grant applications, and agreeing on principles to guide KRAC’s policy. A shared [Google Gemini notebook](https://notebook.google/) let participants ask questions and follow the sources behind the answers.
