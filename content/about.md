We’re rooted in our community. We work with small organizations doing good, building and sharing excellent technology that helps them do more.

## Matt Wynne {#matt}

**Founder**

{{< founder-portrait >}}

Matt Wynne is a software engineer, published author and consultant based in Nelson, British Columbia. He has built his career helping people with different backgrounds understand one another and build useful software together.

Matt has earned his living writing software since the late 1990s. He joined the [Cucumber](https://cucumber.io) open-source project in 2008 and co-founded Cucumber Ltd in 2013, serving as CEO until 2018 and helping grow its consulting and training work. He is a co-author of [*The Cucumber Book*](https://pragprog.com/titles/hwcuc2/the-cucumber-book-second-edition/), [*The Cucumber for Java Book*](https://pragprog.com/titles/srjcuc/the-cucumber-for-java-book/) and [*Cucumber Recipes*](https://pragprog.com/titles/dhwcr/cucumber-recipes/).

He writes software and helps teams work better together through coaching, facilitation and training.

For the past few years, Matt has worked with Silicon Valley startups exploring new ways to build software with AI. He started Impact Works to bring what he’s learned back to his community and put it to work for good.

## Our values {#values}

### Justice

Redistribute access to technology.

Impact Works is as much a political act as it is a business. We put powerful technology in the hands of people and communities the market leaves behind. We refuse work that makes inequality worse.

### Quality

Excellence on your terms.

Good work comes from paying attention: understanding what you’re working with, who you’re doing it for, and why. We aim for work that’s a pleasure to use and affordable to run and maintain.

### Community

We’re part of something bigger.

We’re part of the community we serve, and our customers are our partners. We put the people doing the work first and share what we build so others can use it and keep it going.
