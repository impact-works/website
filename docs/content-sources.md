# About page sources

The biography and values were reviewed and edited with Matt. This file records the public sources and editorial context for future updates.

## Matt Wynne

- https://mattwynne.net/about — professional programming since the late 1990s; joined Cucumber in 2008; co-founded Cucumber Ltd in 2013; consulting and training work; CEO until autumn 2018; developed Example Mapping; now based in Nelson, BC.
- https://mattwynne.net/hire-me — bridging technical and non-technical stakeholders; hands-on technical coaching and collaborative practices.
- https://pragprog.com/titles/hwcuc2/the-cucumber-book-second-edition/ — co-authorship of *The Cucumber Book*, second edition (2017).
- https://pragprog.com/titles/srjcuc/the-cucumber-for-java-book/ — co-authorship of *The Cucumber for Java Book* (2015).
- https://pragprog.com/titles/dhwcr/cucumber-recipes/ — co-authorship of *Cucumber Recipes* (2013).

Matt directly supplied the 2023–2026 Silicon Valley startup and AI experience, and his intention to bring that expertise back to his community through Impact Works. This paragraph is based on his account rather than the public sources above; it does not imply that he relocated to Silicon Valley.

Historical Cucumber roles are written in the past tense. Do not describe Matt as Cucumber's original creator or imply he still leads the project. Older publisher bios place him in Scotland; use his personal biography for his current location.
